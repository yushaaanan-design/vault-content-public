# Week 1 scripts (draft, 2026-09-29)

Rules: styling only, no Fable products on screen, no fake personal experience ("this works because...", never "I bought..."), under 30s, hook in 2 seconds. Post about 9 PM Dhaka time. Caption language chosen per post, log it in the tracker in [[social-content-strategy]]. Always tick the platform's AI label.

## 1. Male — Dressing well in Dhaka heat (styling)
- **Hook (on screen):** "Stop dressing for a cold country."
- **Script:** "Dhaka heat is not the place for heavy fabric. Go for cotton or linen, in light or earthy tones. Keep the fit relaxed, not tight, so air can move. Dark colours absorb heat, so save them for the evening. Dress for the weather and you will look sharper and feel better. Follow for more."
- **CTA:** follow.

## 2. Female — 4 fit mistakes (do's and don'ts)
- **Hook:** "Four fit mistakes that ruin a good outfit."
- **Script:** "One, sleeves that fall past your wrist bone. Two, trousers that pool over your shoes. Three, a shirt too tight across the shoulders. Four, oversized on top and oversized on the bottom. Balance is the whole secret: if one piece is loose, keep the other fitted. Follow for more."
- **CTA:** follow, "save this for your next shop".

## 3. Male — Campus to evening (outfit of the day)
- **Hook:** "One look, two places."
- **Script:** "Start with a clean, neutral shirt and dark trousers. For the day, keep it simple with sleeves rolled once. For the evening, add a jacket and swap to leather shoes. Same base, two moods, no outfit change at home. Follow for more."
- **CTA:** follow.

## 4. Female — Three colours that always work (styling)
- **Hook:** "Three colours that never fail."
- **Script:** "Ivory, black, and a muted gold. These three work together in almost any mix. Ivory is soft, black grounds it, and gold gives one quiet accent. Pick two for the main look and keep the third small. Simple palettes always look expensive. Follow for more."
- **CTA:** follow. (Uses Fable's palette without naming products.)

## Production checklist per video
1. Paste script into the chosen avatar tool with the saved voice.
2. Check lip-sync and skin realism at full screen.
3. Add on-screen hook text and captions (English or Bangla, log which).
4. Export vertical 9:16.
5. Upload with the AI label on to Reels, TikTok, Shorts (Facebook and Instagram).
6. Log views, saves, shares, follows after 48 hours.

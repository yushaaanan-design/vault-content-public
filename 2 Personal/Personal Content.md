# Personal content
Content that is the owner's own: the pitch, ideas, own voice or face, quick experiments ("random content"). Back to [[README]].

The owner's reason for this folder (2026-10-08): a repeatable way to make a video from a script, so a pitch or a random idea can become a finished piece without starting from zero. The animation pipeline in [[3 AI Animation/Pipeline]] is the main helper; the generation tools in [[1 AI Generation/Tools]] are the other.

## Folder contents
- [[2 Personal/Ideas]]: a running list of ideas, newest first.
- [[2 Personal/Pitch]]: the pitch, in versions.
- [[2 Personal/Productions]]: finished pieces.
